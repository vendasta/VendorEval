REQUIREMENTS: Enterprise IT Security & SOC Services — FY2026-27

BACKGROUND:
Our organisation is looking to engage a Managed Security Services Provider (MSSP) to set up and operate a 24x7 Security Operations Centre (SOC), deploy endpoint protection across 2,500+ endpoints, implement SIEM, and provide vulnerability management and incident response services. The engagement is for an initial 2-year term.

MUST-HAVE:
- 24x7x365 SOC monitoring with dedicated analysts
- SIEM deployment and management (cloud-hosted preferred)
- Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR) for 2,500 endpoints (Windows, Mac, Linux)
- Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT) — quarterly
- Incident Response retainer with 1-hour SLA for critical incidents
- Compliance support for ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II
- Dedicated Threat Intelligence feeds
- Monthly executive security reports and dashboards

IMPORTANT:
- Year 1 total cost under INR 1,50,00,000 (1.5 Crore)
- Implementation within 8-10 weeks
- Onshore team (India-based analysts)
- Data residency within India
- Minimum 99.9% SIEM platform uptime
- Integration with existing tools: Microsoft 365, Azure AD, AWS CloudTrail, Jira

NICE-TO-HAVE:
- Security Awareness Training for employees
- Dark web monitoring
- Brand protection and phishing takedown
- Dedicated Slack/Teams channel for real-time alerts
- Tabletop exercises for incident response

EVALUATION CRITERIA:
- Cost: 20%
- Technical Capability & SLA: 25%
- Team & Experience: 20%
- Risk & Compliance: 20%
- Timeline & Implementation: 15%
